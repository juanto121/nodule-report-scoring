How to score
  • Score every item from 1 (fails) to 5 (fully meets); 3 is partly met, for example a section that exists but is incomplete.
  • Judge only against the supplied information above. The AI had no images: anything it states that is not in the supplied information, including normal findings such as “no pleural effusion”, counts as unsupported.
  • Placeholders left for the radiologist (for example “[specify segment]”) and questions to the reader are not statements.
  • Fidelity items are phrased so that 5 means the report is free of the problem.
  • Scored items fold into chips; click a chip to change its score. Everything saves in this browser as you go. When you finish, download or copy the CSV and send it back.
